# Guanxi: return to Shenzhen

A culture-first course for an English-speaking beginner who wants lasting business relationships. Simplified Chinese, pinyin, local examples, original fictional scenes in real places. This is practical foundations and repeated rehearsal, not a fluency guarantee or an HSK/BCT preparation claim.

The recurring fictional cast: Lin Yue (林悦 Lín Yuè), a Nanshan hardware founder; Chen Wei (陈伟 Chén Wěi), a Huaqiangbei supplier with family in Dongguan; Zhou Min (周敏 Zhōu Mǐn), a Shenzhen colleague originally from Chengdu; and the learner, returning to a city they admire. Never present the cast's choices as representative of everyone. The interface reuses the existing Gnothi owl guide and family components. The guide is a teaching character, not a claim about Shenzhen wildlife or cultural symbolism.

|u6 Understand the number|Huaqiangbei; confirm before paying|Numbers and 万; prices; quantities/measure words; payment; clear written recap|
|u7 Visit the team|Nanshan office and Dongguan factory; respect people and process|Roles and introductions; schedule; permission and safety; ask how something works; thank the team|
|u8 Make the meeting useful|Nanshan meeting; ask, listen and clarify|Agenda; opinions; disagree constructively; confirm understanding; action and ownership|
|u9 From sample to order|Huaqiangbei/Dongguan; verify rather than rush|Samples; specifications; quality; delivery; trial order|
|u10 Negotiate for the long term|Supplier discussion; protect clarity and trust|Compare offers; scope and price; payment terms; resolve a constraint; document agreement|
|u11 When something goes wrong|Remote update and return visit; surface bad news early|Describe a problem; clarify evidence; apologize responsibly; propose solutions; follow through|

|u14 Return to Shenzhen|Independent trip; combine skills and notice progress|Arrival and reconnecting; supplier visit; meeting and negotiation; dinner; partnership recap|

Each unit has five lessons of roughly 8–12 items. Words and useful phrases accumulate with sentence chunks and unseen decisions. Numbers, travel logistics and etiquette use fictional details; live rules, timetables and prices must be checked separately. Every local culture card has a read source. Grammar is functional and cumulative: basic SVO/是/吗/不, question words and 的, time before verb/在, 想/要/可以, measure words, 会/能, 了 and 没, comparisons, experience 过, conditional 如果, 把 only in clear concrete beginner examples.

Sound policy: dictionary tones are printed. Spoken third-tone pairs, 不 and 一 changes are separately labeled, never silently mixed. Browser TTS is useful exposure, not a teacher, tone grader or pronunciation assessment. Say-aloud prompts ask for self-assessment. Pinyin support remains available; the listening game conceals it until feedback.

Seven phases: evidence research; curriculum and schema; engine; content writing + separate language and culture reviews + fixes; browser QA; final critique; deploy. Current implementation/review records live in research/reviews. Editorial limitations remain explicit. All examples are original; downloaded sources remain gitignored. No sibling edits, GitHub publication, analytics or external AI API costs.

## Design correction — 2026-11-03

The user explicitly requested the existing product-family design without original design decisions. The current interface therefore uses Gnothi’s exact palette, stylesheet baseline, icons, owl artwork, phone-width frame, three bottom tabs, course path and full-screen lesson components. Only English/Mandarin content, Chinese font fallbacks and bindings to this course’s existing learning behavior are adapted.
