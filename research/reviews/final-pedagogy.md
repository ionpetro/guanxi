## Five highest-impact edits

1. **u1-assess1 tests the next unit.** It requires 姓 (xìng) and 您姓什么 (nín xìng shénme) before those are taught in u2l1. Replace the heldout scene with a missed word/name and make the best response 请再说一遍 (qǐng zài shuō yī biàn), learned in u1l2. Plausible contrasting questions from u1 are 这个字怎么读？ (zhège zì zěnme dú?), which asks how a *written character* is read, and 你是林悦吗？ (nǐ shì Lín Yuè ma?), which guesses an identity.
2. **u1-3-10 does not assess the sounds it names.** The question asks for the generically best learning approach to q/x/c/zh/ü. The adjacent grammar quiz tests only the lip position for ü. A future listening discrimination that plays isolated 七 (qī) and 西 (xī) and hides the written answer until feedback would directly check sound recognition. If the current engine cannot do this, label the card as sound exposure and avoid implying that the scenario measures the distinctions.
3. **u6-1-10 has two reasonable answers.** With conflicting 三百 (sān bǎi) and 三十 (sān shí) notes, both “是三百吗?” and “是三十吗?” can ask for clarification. Make the best option 是三百还是三十？ (shì sān bǎi háishi sān shí?), then use premature assertions as distractors.
4. **u6-3-10 checks only a hypothesis.** “是每个五十元吗?” can get “no” without establishing whether 50 yuan is per pack or total; its other options ask unrelated time/place questions. Use 五十元是每个的价格，还是一共的价格？ (wǔshí yuán shì měi ge de jiàgé, háishi yīgòng de jiàgé?) as the complete clarification and contrast with partial checks about total or pack size.
5. **u6-assess1 gives away the language structure in English.** The prompt says “each box with 十个 parts,” leaving arithmetic as the main task. Use 两盒，每盒十个 (liǎng hé, měi hé shí ge) in the scene and ask for the total piece count. The existing 20/12/2 options then test comprehension plus arithmetic.
